About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate working with Dr. Ali Jannesari at Iowa State University. My current research explores algorithmic and systems-level optimizations for GNNs, aiming to reduce computational and communication overhead while maintaining model accuracy. I am particularly interested in efficient data movement, parallel computing, memory management, and distributed optimization tailored for modern HPC platforms.
