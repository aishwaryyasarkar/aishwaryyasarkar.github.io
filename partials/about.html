About me

As a researcher with a passion for deep learning, I specialize in developing algorithms and models that leverage the latest advances in high-performance computing to accelerate the training of neural networks. My current research focuses on graph neural networks, exploring novel approaches to training these models and optimizing their performance in a distributed computing environment.

Projects

  • MassiveGNN

    A novel prefetch and eviction scheme integrated into Amazon's DistDGL to optimize sampling and communication overheads in distributed Graph Neural Network (GNN) training, achieving a 15-40% performance improvement in Open Graph Banchmark (OGB) graph datasets on NERSC's Perlmutter Supercomputer.

  • HydroDeep

    A knowledge-guided deep neural network for streamflow prediction with gridded (pixelated) spatiotemporal data, that outperformed benchmark models in the application domain Proposed four transfer learning approaches that reduce training time by 95% with accuracy improvement of up to 108%.

  • SnaaP Tag

    Developed a community-based low-cost hybrid energy sensor network system using RFIDs for real-time localization and tracking of objects. Received funding from the Department of Science & Technology, Govt. of India. Patent Filed: 201731014175 (April 2017, India)

  • Cerca Trova

    A real-time rapid response emergency redressal service where an Android Application is designed to bridge the gap between the help seeker and the emergency service provider.

  • TowFi

    Developed a mobile network dead zone warning system, deployed on the IBM Bluemix, which tracks and warns users about network depleted zones in the users’ vicinity using a combination of statistical analysis and interpolation of previously available networks data. Won IBM Bluemix Idea Hack in association with NASSCOM & IAMAI Mobile. Patent Filed: 201731014184 (April 2017, India)

  • Product Recommendation System

    A system that recommends Amazon products to users based on product ratings, only these ratings are predicted by the model itself by analyzing the sentiment from the users’ comments.

  • Smart Toilet

    A system based on Raspberry Pi designed to fit in the toilet which is able to detect blood in urine and stool, with an Android Application that will receive alerts if any unnatural amount of blood is present.
