About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate working with Dr. Ali Jannesari at Iowa State University. My interest lies at the intersection of HPC and machine learning, in optimizing neural networks for large-scale, distributed environments. Particularly, I am interested in designing efficient training strategies that leverage parallel computing, distributed optimization, and novel memory management techniques to enhance scalability and performance.

My current research explores algorithmic and systems-level optimizations for GNNs, aiming to reduce computational overhead while maintaining model accuracy. I am particularly interested in efficient data movement, parallel execution strategies, and optimization techniques tailored for modern HPC platforms.

I hold a Masters in Computer Science from Iowa State University and am actively involved in research efforts that bridge machine learning and large-scale computing.

Research Projects

  • MassiveGNN

    A novel prefetch and eviction scheme integrated into Amazon's DistDGL to optimize sampling and communication overheads in distributed Graph Neural Network (GNN) training, achieving a 15-40% performance improvement in Open Graph Banchmark (OGB) graph datasets on NERSC's Perlmutter Supercomputer. [Code] [CLUSTER'24]

  • Model Souping in GNNs

    Souping strategies for GNNs that enhance efficiency by reducing time and memory overhead. Our methods achieve significant speedups and accuracy improvements across various datasets and architectures. [Code] [IPDPS'25 GrAPL]

  • HydroDeep

    A knowledge-guided deep neural network for streamflow prediction with gridded (pixelated) spatiotemporal data, that outperformed benchmark models in the application domain. Proposed four transfer learning approaches that reduce training time by 95% with accuracy improvement of up to 108%. [NeurIPS'21 AI4S] [HPC ASIA'23 MMCP] [IPDPS'23 ExSAIS]
