Hobbies

Reading

    I enjoy exploring various genres and usually gravitate toward books that make me think, challenge my assumptions or teach me about a new topic or event that I had not encountered before. Some books that have stayed with me are:

  • Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman
  • The Gene: An Intimate History by Siddhartha Mukherjee
  • An Immense World: How Animal Senses Reveal the Hidden Realms Around Us by Ed Yong
  • The Book Thief by Markus Zusak
  • 1984 by George Orwell
  • David Copperfield by Charles Dickens
  • Into Thin Air: A Personal Account of the Mt. Everest Disaster by Jon Krakauer
