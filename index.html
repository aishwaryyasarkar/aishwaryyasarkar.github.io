About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate working with Dr. Ali Jannesari at Iowa State University. My current research explores algorithmic and systems-level optimizations for GNNs, aiming to reduce computational and communication overhead while maintaining model accuracy. I am particularly interested in efficient data movement, parallel computing, memory management, and distributed optimization tailored for modern HPC platforms.

Recent Updates

    Projects

    • NOMAD

      A distributed-memory graph embedding framework using MPI to generate embeddings for massive graphs. NOMAD demonstrates median speedups of 10/100x on CPU-based NERSC Perlmutter cluster relative to the popular reference implementations of multi-threaded LINE and node2vec, 35-76x over distributed PyTorch-BigGraph (PBG), and competitive embedding quality relative to LINE, node2vec, and GraphVite, while yielding 12-370x end-to-end speedups on real-world graphs. [arXiv]

    • Rudder

      LLM agents that adaptively steer prefetching and buffer management in AWS DistDGL to reduce communication overhead in distributed GNN training. Evaluated on NERSC's Perlmutter Supercomputer, Rudder improves end-to-end training performance by up to 91% over baseline DistDGL and reduces communication by over 50%. [Code] [ICS'26]

    • MassiveGNN

      A novel prefetch and eviction scheme integrated into Amazon's DistDGL to optimize sampling and communication overheads in distributed Graph Neural Network (GNN) training, achieving a 15-40% performance improvement in Open Graph Benchmark (OGB) graph datasets on NERSC's Perlmutter Supercomputer. [Code] [CLUSTER'24]

    • Model Souping in GNNs

      Souping strategies for GNNs that enhance efficiency by reducing time and memory overhead. Our methods achieve significant speedups and accuracy improvements across various datasets and architectures. [Code] [IPDPS'25 GrAPL]

    • Scalable Spatiotemporal Forecasting for Hydrology

      Hydrological forecasting using CNNs, LSTMs, GNNs, and Transformers, integrating domain knowledge to improve accuracy, efficiency, and transferability on large-scale river basins, with distributed training for scalable deployment. [NeurIPS'21 AI4S] [HPC ASIA'23 MMCP] [IPDPS'23 ExSAIS] [SIGSPATIAL'25]
